# Cachenet Enterprise CDN Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## Overview

This Service Level Agreement (SLA) defines the performance and availability commitments for Cachenet Enterprise CDN services. Our commitment to enterprise-grade reliability ensures your business can depend on our CDN infrastructure 24/7/365.

## 📊 Performance Guarantees

### Uptime Commitment
- **99.99% Uptime SLA** - Maximum 4.32 minutes of downtime per month
- **99.95% Network Availability** across all edge locations
- **Zero planned downtime** for maintenance (rolling updates)

### Performance Standards
- **<100ms Median Latency** globally from our edge nodes
- **<50ms 95th Percentile Response Time** for cached content
- **>95% Cache Hit Ratio** across all customer content
- **>99% DNS Resolution Success Rate** with <20ms response time

### Bandwidth & Throughput
- **Unlimited Bandwidth** - no throttling or caps
- **10Gbps+ Per Edge Node** capacity with burst capability
- **Automatic DDoS Mitigation** up to 500 Gbps
- **Global Load Distribution** across 50+ edge locations

## 📋 Service Exclusions

### Planned Maintenance
- **Rolling Updates** that don't affect service availability
- **Infrastructure Upgrades** during designated maintenance windows
- **Customer-initiated Changes** that may cause temporary disruption

### External Factors
- **Internet Backbone Issues** beyond our network control
- **DNS Propagation Delays** from customer DNS changes
- **Origin Server Problems** not related to CDN service
- **Force Majeure Events** including natural disasters

### Customer Configuration
- **Misconfigured Origin Servers** affecting content delivery
- **Invalid SSL Certificates** causing connection issues
- **Improper Cache Headers** reducing cache efficiency
- **Traffic Patterns** exceeding normal usage by >1000%

## Legal Terms

### Agreement Scope
This SLA is effective from the date of service activation and remains in effect for the duration of the service agreement. Any modifications to this SLA require written consent from both parties.

### Limitation of Liability
Cachenet's total liability under this SLA shall not exceed the total amount paid by the customer in the 12 months preceding the incident. Service credits represent the customer's sole remedy for SLA breaches.

### Measurement Period
All SLA measurements are calculated on a monthly basis from the first day to the last day of each calendar month, based on Cachenet's monitoring systems.
