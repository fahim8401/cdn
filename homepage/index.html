Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about XenCDN

How is XenCDN different from traditional CDNs?

XenCDN partners directly with ISPs to create edge nodes, eliminating the need for expensive data center infrastructure. This allows us to offer enterprise-grade performance at a fraction of the cost.

What peering options do you offer for ISPs?

We support multiple peering methods: BGP peering at IXPs, direct cross-connects, GRE tunnels for quick setup, and hosted nodes where ISPs run our software on their infrastructure.

How do you ensure performance and reliability?

Our intelligent routing system automatically directs traffic to the best-performing edge node. We maintain strict SLAs with ISP partners and provide real-time monitoring and failover capabilities.

What kind of content do you cache?

We cache static content like HTML, CSS, JS, images, fonts, documents, and videos. We do not cache private content, gaming binaries, or copyrighted material from third-party platforms.

How quickly can I get started?

Clients can start immediately with our free tier. Domain setup takes minutes, and our system automatically issues SSL certificates and configures caching rules. ISP partnerships typically take 2-3 business days for approval.

Do you offer technical support?

Yes! We provide email support for all plans, priority support for Pro customers, and dedicated 24/7 support for Enterprise clients. ISP partners get dedicated technical account management.
